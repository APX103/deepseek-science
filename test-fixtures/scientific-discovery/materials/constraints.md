# Catalyst discovery constraints

We are exploring an oxygen-evolution electrocatalyst for alkaline water electrolysis.

- Avoid iridium and ruthenium because of scarcity.
- Prefer elements with broadly available supply chains.
- Target operation in 1 M KOH near room temperature.
- The immediate output is a falsifiable shortlist, not a claim of a new material.
- These are synthetic local source cards, not real literature. Tie every reported
  performance or stability claim to its source-card ID, never invent a URL, and
  separate reported observations from the agent's inference.
- Propose a small first-pass experiment with appropriate controls and failure criteria.
