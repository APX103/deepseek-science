# Source A — NiFe-LDH on nickel foam

- Electrolyte: 1 M KOH, nominally 25 °C.
- Geometric current density: 10 mA cm^-2 at 270 mV overpotential.
- Stability: 20 h at 10 mA cm^-2; voltage drift +7 mV.
- Compensation: 85% iR correction was applied.
- Electrode: catalyst grown directly on porous nickel foam.
- Replicates: three preparations; reported spread ±18 mV.
- Caveat stated by the team: the nickel-foam contribution was not isolated with
  a bare-foam control.
