# Source C — attempted NiFe-LDH replication

- Electrolyte: rigorously iron-depleted 1 M KOH, 25 °C.
- Geometric current density: 10 mA cm^-2 at 338 mV overpotential.
- Stability: 12 h at 10 mA cm^-2; voltage drift +19 mV.
- Compensation: no iR correction.
- Electrode: glassy carbon disk; loading 0.20 mg cm^-2.
- Replicates: five films; reported spread ±25 mV.
- Additional observation: adding 1 ppm dissolved iron improved the median
  overpotential to 294 mV in a follow-up check (two films only).
- Caveat stated by the team: substrate, loading, compensation, and iron purity
  all differ from Source A, so this is not a clean direct contradiction.
