# Source B — cobalt spinel on titanium mesh

- Electrolyte: 1 M KOH, 24–27 °C.
- Geometric current density: 10 mA cm^-2 at 305 mV overpotential.
- Stability: 100 h at 10 mA cm^-2; voltage drift +11 mV.
- Compensation: no iR correction.
- Electrode: oxide ink on titanium mesh; loading 0.8 mg cm^-2.
- Replicates: four electrodes; reported spread ±12 mV.
- Caveat stated by the team: cobalt supply and handling are less attractive than
  iron/manganese-rich candidates, although no precious metal was used.
