# Source D — manganese-cobalt oxide at elevated temperature

- Electrolyte: 1 M KOH, 60 °C.
- Geometric current density: 10 mA cm^-2 at 282 mV overpotential.
- Stability: 200 h at 10 mA cm^-2; voltage drift +24 mV.
- Compensation: full post-hoc iR correction.
- Electrode: oxide on stainless-steel mesh; loading not reported.
- Replicates: one long-duration electrode; no spread reported.
- Caveat stated by the team: the elevated temperature and missing loading make
  the headline overpotential non-comparable with room-temperature cards.
